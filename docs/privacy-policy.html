Privacy policy

HardTalkAI · Career coaching practice · Last updated 19 September 2026

This is a short, honest policy for a prototype. It is not legal advice and it does not claim GDPR/CCPA certification, a Data Protection Officer, or a production-grade retention program.

Contact: hello@hardtalk.ai. If that inbox is not yet monitored, use the repo owner: aashish2k2@gmail.com.

What we collect (when you practice)

The app does not create accounts. There is no sign-in, profile, or email capture in this build.

When you run a typed drill, the client sends to our coaching API (POST /api/chat):

  • the scenario id you picked
  • the text you typed (your practice reply)
  • the conversation history for that round

We use that payload only to score the turn and generate the next counterpart line. Coaching scores and tips are computed on our server. The catalog is loaded from GET /api/scenarios.

Optional Android counterpart call

If you tap Call on Android and grant the microphone, the app mints a short-lived OpenAI Realtime client secret (POST /api/voice/session), streams microphone audio over WebRTC directly to OpenAI, then sends the transcript to POST /api/voice/complete so we can score up to three user utterances. Coach Heather is not on the call — she recaps after hang-up. Typed practice does not use the microphone. We do not keep a recording on our servers.

This prototype does not write practice chats to a database. Requests are handled in memory. Ordinary web-server logs may still record IP address, time, and URL path.

Optional OpenAI

If the operator sets OPENAI_API_KEY on the server, typed counterpart replies are generated by OpenAI (default gpt-4o-mini unless OPENAI_MODEL is set), and live calls use OpenAI Realtime. In those cases the scenario prompt, history, message, and (for a call) audio are sent to OpenAI. If no key is set, the server uses a built-in reply, Call is hidden, and the server does not call OpenAI.

Coaching scores and tips are not sent to OpenAI.

OpenAI’s handling of that data is governed by OpenAI’s privacy policy.

What we do not collect / do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not run ads, attribution SDKs, or analytics packages in this build.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone, location, contacts, or photos. Android Call asks for the microphone only when you start a counterpart call.
  • Android may take a Play payment to unlock HardTalk Pro (the paid catalog). That purchase is processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not create an account for you. The raise drill stays free. Web and the public Static Space demo are not paywalled.
  • Practice messages are not posted publicly and are not shown to other users.
  • This is a workplace-coaching prototype aimed at adults. Do not use it if you are under 13.

Android’s INTERNET permission is used to reach the coaching API and, for a live call, OpenAI Realtime. RECORD_AUDIO is used only for the optional counterpart call. Debug builds may use HTTP on a local network; a store build should use HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

Because this prototype does not store chats in a database, there is usually nothing to delete on our side after the request finishes. If you believe a log or hosted environment kept something of yours, email hello@hardtalk.ai or aashish2k2@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page when the product persists data, adds accounts, analytics, or a production host. Last updated 19 September 2026.
